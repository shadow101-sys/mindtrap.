MINDTRAP AUDIO

Put licensed audio files in this folder:
- background-music.mp3: optional; the header Music button plays or pauses it
- start.mp3: round-start sound effect
- turn.mp3: turn sound effect
- vote.mp3: intentionally blank until a vote sound is supplied
- result.mp3: round-results sound effect

The Mute SFX / Unmute SFX button controls sound effects. The music control is separate.

Optional sound-effect slots:
- The turn and vote effects remain disabled in public/app.js until their files are available.
- Keep the files small enough for your Git host and deployment service.
